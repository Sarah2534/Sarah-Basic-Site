Egg Noodles and Kielbasa

A cheap and quick meal for busy days.

Back to Home Biscuits and Gravy | Egg Noodles and Kielbasa | Steak and Mashed Potatoes

About This Recipe

This recipe is simple, affordable and doesn't take a lot of time to make. It is a great option when you want a filling meal without spending too much money.

Ingredients

  • 1 package of egg noodles (12 oz.)
  • 1 package of kielbasa sausage
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil.
  2. Add the egg noodles and cook according to the package directions.
  3. Drain the noodles and set them aside.
  4. Cut the kielbasa into small slices.
  5. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
  6. Add the kielbasa and cook until lightly browned.
  7. Add the garlic powder, salt and black pepper.
  8. Add the cooked egg noodles to the skillet and stir everything together.
  9. Cook for another 2-3 minutes until heated through.
  10. Top with Parmesan cheese if desired and serve warm.
